QuickBooks, Ramp, Bill.com & Payroll Integrations

We work inside the accounting, payments, and payroll tools you already use — keeping everything connected, accurate, and in sync for Minnesota small businesses.

Accounting platforms

QuickBooks

Whether you run in the cloud or on a powerful desktop install, we keep your QuickBooks file clean, current, and reconciled.

QuickBooks Online

Cloud-based bookkeeping with anywhere access. We manage your chart of accounts, transactions, reconciliations, and reporting directly in QuickBooks Online so your books are always up to date. Not sure which version fits? Read our QuickBooks Online vs. Desktop Enterprise comparison.

QuickBooks Desktop Enterprise

Robust, feature-rich accounting for growing and more complex businesses. We work within your Desktop Enterprise file for advanced job costing, class tracking, and detailed reporting.

Payments & bill management

Accounts Payable & Receivable

Modern AP/AR platforms let us pay bills, collect from customers, and control spend — with a clean audit trail that flows straight into your books.

Ramp.com

Corporate cards and spend management that streamline expenses and automate receipt capture. We use Ramp to keep spending controlled, coded, and reconciled against your books. See why we recommend Ramp company cards for small businesses.

Bill.com

Automated accounts payable and receivable with approval workflows and digital payments. We manage vendor bills and customer invoices through Bill.com for faster, cleaner cash flow.

Payroll partners

Payroll

We integrate with the leading payroll providers so wages, taxes, and filings stay accurate and posted correctly to your books.

ADP

Full-service payroll and HR for businesses of every size.

Paychex

Payroll, tax administration, and benefits for small business.

Gusto

Modern, easy payroll and benefits built for small teams.

UKG

Workforce and payroll management for more complex needs.

Already using one of these tools?

We'll plug right into your existing setup — no need to switch platforms. Let's connect your tools to clean, reliable books.
