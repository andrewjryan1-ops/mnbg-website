Bookkeeping & Controller Services for Minnesota Small Businesses

Ten services that keep your books accurate, your reporting clear, and your business moving forward — handled by a dependable local team serving Minneapolis, St. Paul and all of Minnesota.

Need more? See our controller & month-end close, fractional CFO and catch-up bookkeeping services.

Month after month

Everything we take off your plate

1Data Entry, Accounts Payable & Check Runs

Entering your vendor invoices and coding them to the correct expense account — and to a customer or job account when required. We cut checks and process bank check orders per your approval, working in QuickBooks and AP platforms like Ramp and Bill.com.

2Accounts Receivable Invoicing & Statements

Creating your customer invoices for your review and sending them to your customers and clients. We follow up on invoices to make sure they get paid and review the A/R report to keep cash flow coming in.

3Monthly Account Reconciliation

Balancing checkbooks, savings accounts, and credit cards against bank statements — plus balancing any other miscellaneous accounts so everything ties out.

4Month-End Reports

Preparing month-end financial reports to meet bank requirements, for your review, and to submit to your CPA for tax planning. Includes Budget vs. Actual income and expense reporting.

5Month-to-Month Analysis

Comparing the current month's statements with the prior month, the same month last year, and year-to-date vs. prior year-to-date. We track sales and expense history and goal accountability so we know what to adjust to make you more profitable.

6Job Costing & Class Coding

Tracking expenses to a job or customer so you know the profit of each project — and whether to adjust your estimates. Class coding tracks expenses by product type, so you understand which product and service lines are most profitable and most sold.

7Payroll

Owner's payroll, employee payroll service, and full payroll services — handled accurately and on schedule, integrated with ADP, Paychex, Gusto and UKG.

8Business Forms & Audits

Preparing forms and packages for bank loans and similar needs, and preparing workers' compensation insurance documentation.

9Business Support

Helping you set business goals for the year or quarter and build your business plan — and serving as your accountability partner in reaching them. We maintain an approved vendor list of business-supporting companies, build budgets, and identify your top customers by percentage of profit.

10Year-End Review Service

Reviewing the books to ensure everything is tied out for taxes and that you're maximizing your deductions before year-end.

Common questions

Bookkeeping questions we hear most

How much does small-business bookkeeping cost in Minnesota?

It depends on your transaction volume, the number of accounts we reconcile, and whether you need accounts payable, receivable or payroll handled too. We quote a monthly fee after a short conversation about your business, so there are no hourly surprises. Tell us about your business and we'll put a number to it.

Do you work in QuickBooks Online or QuickBooks Desktop?

Both. We work in QuickBooks Online and QuickBooks Desktop Enterprise, and we can help you decide which fits your business if you're choosing between them. See our integrations page for the payroll, AP and AR tools we connect to.

My books are months behind. Can you still help?

Yes, and there's no judgment involved. Our catch-up and cleanup bookkeeping service reconciles every past month, fixes miscoded transactions and gets you tax-ready before monthly bookkeeping takes over.

Do you replace my CPA?

No. We keep your books accurate and reconciled all year and prepare the year-end package for your tax preparer, so tax season is faster and cheaper. If you need forward-looking help with cash flow, budgets or financing, our fractional CFO services fill that gap.

Do you only work with businesses in the Twin Cities?

We're based in Minnesota and work with small businesses across the state, including the Minneapolis–St. Paul metro. Most of the work happens in your accounting platform, so location is rarely a barrier.

Not sure which services you need?

Tell us about your business and we'll recommend the right mix — from monthly bookkeeping to full-service support.
