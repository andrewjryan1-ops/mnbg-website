Process Improvement & Automation for Minnesota Small Businesses

Special projects that fix the way your accounting gets done, not just the numbers it produces. We find the manual, error-prone steps in your workflow, then redesign and automate them — so your team gets hours back every week and your books get more reliable.

When the process is the problem

Some problems aren't in the numbers. They're in how the numbers get made.

Every growing business accumulates them: the spreadsheet only one person understands, the figures re-keyed into QuickBooks by hand every week, the approval that lives in somebody's inbox, the month-end that takes ten days because three people are waiting on each other. None of it shows up as a line item. It just quietly eats hours and introduces mistakes.

This is a project service, not a monthly retainer. We map how the work actually gets done today, redesign it, automate the repetitive parts, document it and hand it to your team — with a fixed price agreed before any of it starts.

A real project

Weekly subcontractor pay for a Minnesota moving company

Before

The company's twelve subcontracted crews each built their own weekly pay statement in a shared Google Doc. The owner then reviewed every one — running his own manual calculation for each sub, comparing it against what the sub had entered, and correcting the differences by hand. The resulting bills were re-keyed into QuickBooks.

It took about ten hours a week, it depended on two separate sets of manual arithmetic agreeing with each other, and the subs carried the data-entry burden with penalties for late or incorrect submissions.

After

The process is inverted. The company now prepares every statement automatically from its own pay data, and the subcontractors simply review and approve.

The weekly workload dropped from about ten hours to four at most, and the duplicate manual calculations that produced the errors are gone entirely — there is now one source of the numbers instead of two that had to be reconciled by hand.

What the system does

  • Builds each subcontractor's weekly statement automatically from the company's own pay data — every job, calculation, tip and deduction — as a clean, professional PDF
  • Emails each subcontractor a secure link to review their statement online
  • Lets subcontractors approve or request a change in one click. No data entry on their end, no email chains, and every request tracked
  • Routes change requests to the bookkeeper for a decision, then automatically issues a revised statement for the sub to approve
  • Finalizes approved statements into a signed invoice PDF, sent to the subcontractor for their records
  • Produces a QuickBooks-ready bill file, coded to the right vendor, account and class — no re-keying
  • Sends the owner one weekly summary of every approved statement for payment sign-off
  • Keeps a complete audit trail of who approved what, and when

The result: six or more hours back every week, no more double calculations, no re-keying into QuickBooks — and subcontractors went from building their own statements to simply reviewing them.

Built on tools you already know

No new software for your team to learn

We work with the systems small businesses already run on — Excel, QuickBooks Online and Microsoft 365 — and connect them with secure automated workflows, web-based approvals and email delivery. Nothing to replace, nothing new to train everyone on, and no subscription that holds your process hostage.

Common questions

Questions we hear about process work

How much does a process improvement project cost?

Every project is quoted as a fixed price in writing before any work begins, after we have walked through your current process and know what we are actually dealing with. There is no hourly meter and no open-ended engagement. As a rough sense of scale, most projects run four to twelve weeks from first conversation to fully live, depending on how many systems are involved. Tell us what the process looks like now and we will come back with a plan and a number.

Do I have to switch accounting systems?

No. We do not replace your accounting system — we build around QuickBooks and the tools you already use. The work is connecting and streamlining what you have, not migrating you onto something new. See the platforms we work in.

What happens if the new process does not work?

We don't switch you over until it's proven. New processes run alongside the old one first, so you can compare the two on real data before anything is turned off. That's how the subcontractor pay project below was built — in stages, running in parallel, then switched over completely once it held up.

Who owns what you build?

You do. We don't sell software subscriptions, and there is no licence that stops working if you stop working with us. What we build for your business belongs to your business.

What do you build with?

The tools small businesses already run on — Excel, QuickBooks Online and Microsoft 365 — connected with secure automated workflows, web-based approvals and email delivery. No new software for your team to learn, and nothing to replace.

Is this only for existing bookkeeping clients?

No. Process work is a project with a defined beginning and end, and plenty of businesses start there. If we also end up doing your monthly bookkeeping afterwards, that is a separate conversation and entirely up to you.

What is eating your team's week?

Tell us which process is the painful one. We'll walk it with you and come back with a plan and a fixed quote.
